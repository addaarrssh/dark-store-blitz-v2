How to play

  1. Stock your store. Your team decides which shelf each of the 24 products sits on.
  2. Walk the order. Tap the map to walk from START, stand next to each item to grab it, then walk back to START.
  3. Hand in. Fewer steps means more points. Be quick: the first teams get a bonus.
