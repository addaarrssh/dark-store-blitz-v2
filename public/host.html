      How to play

      Only on this screen, only before Round 1. Phones do not show hints while you play.

      Controls help

      Keyboard

      • Space Pause or resume the clock.
      • N Next step: the yellow button in the bottom bar. You are always asked first.
      • Enter Yes in a question box. Esc is no. Pressing N again does nothing.
      • R Show or hide the rules sheet (lobby only).
      • F Full screen on or off.
      • Esc Close a box or the control room.

      Bottom bar

      • Start Round 1 Opens the warehouse on every phone and starts the clock. Pick the length first. You are asked first.
      • Show rules Lobby only. Puts the placement rules, the penalty points and the scoring on this screen so you can explain them. It closes by itself when Round 1 starts. During the game neither this screen nor the phones show the placement rules.
      • Lock joining / Open joining Stops (or allows) new teams. Phones of teams already in can always come back.
      • +1 min, −1 min, +30 s Add or remove time on the running clock. You are asked first if −1 min would end the round.
      • Pause clock / Resume clock Freezes the clock for everybody. Teams can still work and hand in.
      • Show sales per hour Shows how often each product is sold, here and on the phones.
      • Lock layouts now Ends Round 1 early. Mistakes in the layouts are counted now.
      • Reopen layouts for 5 min Only before the first order. Lets teams fix their store.
      • Start order / Next Sends the next order to every phone and starts its clock. You are asked first ("Start Order 4?"), so a slip on the key or a double click cannot skip ahead.
      • End order now Closes the order early. Teams that did not hand in get 0 for it.
      • Replay best run Shows the best route of the round again on the map.
      • Final results Ends the game and opens the podium. You are asked first. Reveal 3rd, 2nd and 1st one by one (asked each time). Phones show each place at the same moment, not before.
      • Download results (CSV) Saves the final table as a file you can open in Excel.

      Control room

      • Jump to a round Start any round. If it was played before you are asked whether to wipe its old scores and play it again.
      • Clock on / off With the clock off, rounds only end when you end them. Switching it back on restarts the clock of the running round.
      • Practice mode Practice only: teams can retry an order as often as they like. Keep it OFF in the competition.
      • Message to every phone A short line shown on all phones and on this screen. Clear takes it away.
      • ± Points Add or take away points for one team, with a reason the team can see.
      • Redo Deletes one team's result for the current order so it can hand in again (for disputes).
      • New PIN For a team that forgot its PIN: type 4 new digits for them. Their connected phones stay in.
      • Remove Takes a team out of the game for good.
      • ⚠ too fast Shown next to a result that came in faster than a person can tap. It got no speed bonus.
      • Reset the whole game Wipes all teams and scores. You must type RESET.

      Good to know

      • If the game laptop restarts in the middle of a round, the clock comes back paused. Press Resume.
      • The dot at the top right is green when this screen is live. If it turns pink, the screen keeps the last state and reconnects by itself.
      • Sounds need one click or key press on this page first.
